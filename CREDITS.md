# Credits and source terms

The original Aura Tunnel source and arrangements are by Daniel Amini, MIT.
The pinned licence is `assets/reference/LICENSE`.

The Amiga fighters use **Mustermann 2** and **Jones** from **Bad Company**
by Puffolotti Accident, released under **CC0 1.0**. Primary pages, exact download
URLs and original-file SHA-256 values are in `assets/fighters/SOURCE.md`.
The build selects 56 Mustermann poses (including nine additional sweep frames)
and 47 Jones poses, crops fixed canvases and converts to three opaque colours
per character. Independent compressed poses become 48×56 hardware sprites;
mirroring supplies the opponent's facing direction. R retains an original-art
comparison using the prior 47-pose Mustermann pair. The complete original packs
are archived separately for future projects, outside this demo's disk payload.
No Konami artwork is included in the native Amiga payload. The original
Spectrum repository documents its Konami-derived reference and separate terms;
that sprite binary is excluded from this repository.

The runner uses CMU Graphics Lab motion capture clip 09_01, converted to BVH by
Bruce Hahne. The data was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created
with funding from NSF EIA-0196217. Original provenance and reuse terms are retained
in `assets/reference/assets/mocap/SOURCE.md` and `READMEFIRST.txt`.

Music retains the Spectrum show's four themes: traditional Korobeiniki,
Neon Drive by this project, Beethoven's Ode to Joy, and the project's Night Flight.
The first and third melodies are public domain. The arrangements and other themes
are covered by the source MIT licence. The Amiga version uses procedural sampled
instruments through Paula's four DMA voices, which accounts for its different sound.

The small HUD alphabet is original code in `tools/hud_font.py`. Pillow is a
build-time image processing dependency. Large outlined lettering comes
from the pinned project's vector strokes. Sunset, galaxy, planet, terrain and
original train strips are generated by this repository. Optional sourced train
strips are adapted from the CC0 artwork credited below.

The build uses vasm, and emulation uses FS-UAE with its internal AROS replacement
ROM. These are external development tools; their binaries are not committed.
Research links and the decision not to import third-party engine code are recorded
in `docs/implementation-plan.md`.

Night Train's optional sourced artwork uses FisherG's **Parallax City Night
(4 Colors)**, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/parallax-city-night-4-colors
(`assets/art/sCityFar.png`, `sCityMid.png`, `sCityClose.png`), and mrti14's **Pixel Train Scene**,
CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-train-scene (`pixel-train.png`).
The build composites the city layers and scales the train, then converts both
to two-plane parallax artwork; R switches to the original
procedural backdrop. Original downloaded PNGs are retained for provenance.
