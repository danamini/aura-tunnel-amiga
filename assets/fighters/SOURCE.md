# Fighter artwork provenance

Both source animations are by **Puffolotti**, credited as **Puffolotti Accident**,
and are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Their original source pages explicitly link CC0 in the Licence(s) field.
The project's MIT licence covers its own code and transformations; it does not
replace the source artwork's CC0 dedication.

## Mustermann 2

- Source: [Fighting character template Mustermann 2](https://opengameart.org/node/97941).
- Original download: [1_8.gif](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/1_8.gif).
- Local filename: `mustermann.gif`.
- Size: 1,890,898 bytes; 229 × 238 pixels; 997 frames.
- SHA-256: `14f224bdd947c12c8f78749713ddce3cf63a2ae7a16d0417c0329595819db625`.

This is the Anthony Nagasaki character template, not a pack of 997 distinct moves
or characters. The source page also offers a second handedness/view variant.
The build selects frames, crops and scales the artwork, quantises its palette,
and converts it into native Amiga sprite data. Runtime mirroring supplies the
opposite facing direction. The authoritative current frame selection is in the
asset generator rather than duplicated here.

## Jones

- Source: [Bad Company assorted military thugs](https://opengameart.org/content/bad-company-assorted-military-thugs-universal-prototype-2-for-scrolling-beat-em-up-or-mugen).
- Original download: [jones_selection_01_karatecretin_sequence.gif](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/jones_selection_01_karatecretin_sequence.gif).
- Local filename: `jones.gif`.
- Size: 296,672 bytes; 174 × 115 pixels; 375 frames.
- SHA-256: `aa37ad1be4a2b0b383ec3750234fe3a402ede9adcbf8452c8572ef404956c86c`.

Jones is a separately named character from the creator's military-thug set.
The downloaded file is the creator's curated karate selection, not the complete
5,370-plus-cel template. Its selected poses receive the same crop, scale, palette
and native-sprite conversion treatment as Mustermann.

The creator appreciates credit but does not require it under the stated CC0
terms. Credit is retained here and in the project's CREDITS.md. No Konami fighter
artwork is included in these source files.
